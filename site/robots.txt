# separate agents now and they are not interchangeable: GPTBot and ClaudeBot

# pair is the only part that is arguably worth anything, and it costs the other
# four — several of which read a Disallow written for a sibling agent as a
# reason to skip the site — so a pre-launch company with six pages and nothing
# proprietary on them gains nothing and loses the citations.
#
# If that trade ever changes, disallow the training agents by name here. Do not
